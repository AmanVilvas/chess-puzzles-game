Describe: En passant, If a pawn moves two squares forward from its starting position and lands next to an opponent’s pawn, that pawn may capture it as if it had moved only one square.
This capture must be done immediately on the very next move, otherwise the right to capture en passant is lost.

Example: Suppose, White has a pawn on d5. Black moves a pawn from c7 to c5 (two squares). White can capture en passant by moving the pawn from d5 to c6, capturing the pawn that moved to c5.
Even though the pawn is on d5, it is captured as if it had moved only to c6.
[...] 1.dxc6
